Think Before You Click

Random links and QR codes can lead to scams, malware, and stolen data.

Why random links are dangerous

Phishing

Fake websites trick you into entering passwords, emails, or payment details.

Malware

Some links automatically download harmful software to your device.

Fake Logins

Attackers copy real websites to steal your accounts.

QR Codes are not always safe

Hidden destination

You cannot see the link behind a QR code before scanning it.

Sticker attacks

Attackers place malicious QR stickers over real ones.

Fake payments

QR codes can redirect payments to criminals.

How to stay safe

Check the URL

Always verify the website address before entering information.

Do not trust random QR codes

Especially in public places like posters or parking meters.

Use security tools

Modern browsers and antivirus tools help detect malicious websites.
